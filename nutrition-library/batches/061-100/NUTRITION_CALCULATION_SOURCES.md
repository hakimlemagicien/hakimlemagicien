# Nutrition Calculation Sources

Ingredient-level calculations use representative generic-food values from USDA FoodData Central (https://fdc.nal.usda.gov/), expressed per 100 g edible portion and matched to the named preparation state. Recipe totals are quantity-weighted and rounded per serving. Calories are summed independently from source kcal values; macro energy uses 4/4/9 and is checked against ingredient energy.

Acceptance threshold used by the Pilot: absolute macro-energy vs ingredient-energy difference ≤15%. Batch maximum: 9.8%.

Local brands, drained yield, cooking moisture, and formulation can change results. A registered nutrition professional must validate production publication and local product substitutions.
